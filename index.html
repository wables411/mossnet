MOSSAWRETTES

    X or B: close

    CONTROLS

    Title
    A or tap starts. The beetle plays on the way in; any button skips it.
    Menu
    D-pad moves, A selects, B goes back to the title, X toggles sound.
    Gallery
    D-pad moves, A opens a piece, B returns to the menu, L and R also move the cursor. ALL shows every Mossawrette read live from Ethereum; YOURS shows the ones in your connected wallet.
    Piece
    A showcase (picture only, A or B to leave). X info card (D-pad scrolls it). Y green mode, a four-shade Game Boy rendering. L and R go to the previous and next piece. B goes back.
    Chart
    B returns to the menu.
    Always
    START: title screen. SELECT: this screen. WALLET pill: connect or disconnect an Ethereum wallet.
    Keyboard
    Arrows, Enter, Escape, X, Y, Q and E for L and R, S for START, C for SELECT.

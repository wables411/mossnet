Data

Species list, names, taxonomy, record counts and regional distribution come from GBIF, the Global Biodiversity Information Facility, aggregating records published by iNaturalist, Tropicos (Missouri Botanical Garden), university herbaria, national biodiversity databases and thousands of individual recorders. GBIF data is used under the licence terms of each contributing dataset. Please cite GBIF.org if you reuse the data.

Photographs

Every photograph in the game is shared by its photographer under a Creative Commons licence (or is in the public domain) that allows reuse with attribution. Most were found through GBIF and iNaturalist; some come from Wikimedia Commons, including a few historical botanical plates. Each in-game journal entry shows the photographer's name and licence.
